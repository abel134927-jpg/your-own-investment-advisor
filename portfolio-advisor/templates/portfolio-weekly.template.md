### 7.2 Guardrails to enforce

- Do not recommend action for drift below `target_allocation.ignore_drift_below` (portfolio-config.yaml).
- Weekly turnover above `risk_limits.weekly_turnover_warning` must be red-flagged.
- New positions should have a cooldown of `risk_limits.new_position_cooldown_weeks`–`new_position_cooldown_max_weeks` weeks unless thesis breaks.
- Crypto Satellite: no active trading recommendation in v1.
